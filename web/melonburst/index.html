        Settings

        Your name and settings stay in this browser. Signing in with Google saves your stats and lets a host save crew scores.

        How to play

        There’s a hidden word, shown as blanks. Guess it one letter at a time: tap a key or type on your keyboard.

        • A right letter fills in every place it appears.
        • A wrong letter snaps another rubber band round your watermelon. It gets squeezed, nervous, then cracks.
        • Your melon survives a set number of bands (6 unless the room changes it). One more and it bursts, and that word scores nothing.

        Points

        Solving a word is worth 10, plus 1 for every band you had to spare. In a race, the first three to solve also get 3, 2 and 1 extra.

        Race

        The computer picks a word and everyone guesses it at the same time, each on their own melon. You can see how the others are doing (how many letters, how many bands) but not their letters.

        Pick words

        Two sides, 1 vs 1 or 2 vs 2. Each round, each side picks a secret word for the other side (in teams, teammates take turns picking). Then everyone guesses the other side’s word on their own melon, and gets to watch the other side struggle with theirs. Team points add up.

        Words must be real English words of 4 to 12 letters. Stuck for one? Press Suggest one.

        On your own

        • Daily word: the same everyday word for everyone today. One go, then share your result.
        • Practice: word after word, as long as you like. Keep your streak going.
