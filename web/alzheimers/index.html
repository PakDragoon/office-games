          How to play

          2 players, one against the other.

          1. Write. You each write 12 things for the other to draw (2 minutes). Things you can doodle quickly work best: an animal, a food, an object. Fill the rest for me adds random ones, and anything not written in time is filled in for you.
          2. Draw. After 3, 2, 1 your opponent’s words appear one at a time, each for a moment (1, 1.5 or 2 seconds, the host picks), and a box on your sheet lights up. Doodle the word in that box. A new word comes every 5, 8 or 12 seconds. After the last word you get 10 more seconds, and you can add to any box until then.
          3. Remember. Now the words are gone and only your doodles are left. The 12 words come back one at a time in a random order: tap the box you drew each one in. You see straight away if you were right.

          Winning

          One point for every right box. The most points wins the round; if it’s a tie, the one who answered faster wins. At the end you see both sheets with the words written under each box.
