                Settings

                Your name and settings stay in this browser. Signing in with Google saves your stats and lets a host save crew scores.

                How to play

                2 to 8 players. Everyone gets a secret card, like a sticky note on their forehead: everyone else can read it, you can’t. Your card shows as ? on your screen. The host picks a deck (Animals, Food, Everyday objects, Jobs, Famous people or Numbers 1 to 100).

                On your turn

                1. Ask a yes-or-no question about your card (up to 100 characters), like “Is it bigger than a car?”. You have 45 seconds, or your turn is skipped.
                2. Everyone else answers: Yes, No, Sort of or Don’t know. In the Numbers deck the answers are Yes, No, Higher and Lower (ask “Is it 50?” and they say Higher or Lower). They have 20 seconds. The most common answer is highlighted for everyone.
                3. Then you guess or pass (30 seconds). A wrong guess shows as “Not it” and ends your turn, same as passing.

                Turns go round the table in seat order. Anyone who has gone away or left is skipped, and the game never waits for them. Every question and its answers stay in the Questions list, so you can look back at what was asked about each player.

                Guessing

                Capital letters, accents, spaces and punctuation don’t matter, and common other names count (a cat is also a kitty). Small typos are forgiven in longer answers. Numbers must be exact. You can give up any time to show your card for 0 points.

                Points

                • A right guess: 12 minus the questions you asked, but never less than 2.
                • First right guess of the round: 2 extra.
                • Give up or run out of time: 0.

                The round ends when everyone has guessed or given up, or when the host’s round timer runs out (5, 8 or 12 minutes, or off). Then every card is shown. Scores carry over to the next round in the same room.

                If someone types your card into a question or guess, it’s hidden from you so it can’t spoil it.
