        Settings

        Your name and settings stay in this browser. Signing in with Google saves your stats and lets a host save crew scores.

        How to play

        Each round one player picks a letter. Everyone then races to fill in every category with a word that starts with that letter: a name, a place, an animal, and so on.

        The round

        • Players take turns picking. The picker has 10 seconds to tap a letter, then the game picks one at random. Letters already used are greyed out, and Q, X and Z stay out unless the host turns on hard letters.
        • Type an answer in every box. The time limit is the host's choice: 60, 90 or 120 seconds.
        • The first player to fill every box can press Zap!. Everyone else then gets 10 more seconds, then the round locks. Once every box is filled you can also press I'm done; with blanks you play on until the round locks.
        • The clock is on the server. Answers that arrive after the lock don't count.

        Scoring

        • 10 points for a valid answer nobody else gave. 5 points if another player gave the same one. 0 for blank, or an answer that doesn't start with the letter or gets turned down.
        • Capitals, spaces and accents don't matter: "Zébra" and "zebra" are the same answer.
        • Answers on the game's lists are accepted straight away. Anything else goes to a quick vote: everyone else taps 👍 or 👎 (up to 60 seconds; it ends as soon as everyone has voted). A majority of 👍, or a tie, accepts it. Nobody votes on their own answers.

        The game

        The host picks the categories (4 to 8), the time, and 5, 10 or all the letters. Points add up over the rounds, and the highest total wins. Ties are broken by the number of 10-point answers. Join in the middle of a game and you watch until the next one.
