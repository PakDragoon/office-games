              Settings

              Your name and settings stay in this browser. Signing in with Google saves your stats and lets a host save crew scores.

              How to play

              2 to 8 players sit round a table. One of you is holding a banana that’s going off.

              Your turn

              When the banana comes to you, you get 2 or 3 letters, like AN. Type any word that has those letters together, like bANana or plANet, and press Enter. The banana passes to the next player with new letters.

              A word can only be played once (plurals and -ing forms count as the same word unless the host changes it).

              Splat

              The banana splats at a random moment the room can’t see. It browns as it ages, which is a clue, not a clock. Whoever is holding it loses a life, and the banana moves on. At 0 lives you’re out and watch the rest. The last player standing wins.

              Bonus life

              Use every letter of the alphabet across your words (the letters panel shows what you still need) and you get a life back.
