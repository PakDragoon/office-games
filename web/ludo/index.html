            How to play

            2 to 4 players each have a colour, a yard in their corner and (usually) four tokens. Everyone races their tokens once round the board, clockwise, then up their own coloured lane to the middle. The first to bring every token home wins.

            1. Roll. On your turn press Roll (or Space). The server rolls the die, so it’s fair for everyone.
            2. Get out. A token leaves the yard onto your start square only on a 6 (or a 1 or 6, if the host chose that).
            3. Move. Pick a token and it moves that many squares. Tokens you can move have a ring; hover or tap one to see where it would land. If only one move is possible it’s played for you.
            4. Capture. Land on a rival’s token and it goes back to its yard. Start squares and squares with a star are safe: nobody can be captured there.
            5. Roll again after a 6, a capture or getting a token home. Three 6s in a row and your turn is over.
            6. Get home. You need the exact number to reach the middle.

            House rules (the host picks)

            • Tokens each: 2 or 3 make a quicker game.
            • Time per turn: when it runs out the computer rolls or moves for you. Three times in a row and it keeps playing for you until you tap I’m back.
            • 2 vs 2: partners sit opposite and never capture each other. Once your tokens are home you roll for your partner and move their tokens. A team wins when both are home.
            • Blocks: two tokens of one colour on a square form a wall rivals can’t pass or land on.
            • Capture before home: your tokens keep going round until you’ve captured someone.
            • Every place: the game carries on until only one player is left, so everyone gets a place.

            Bots: the host can add computer players in the lobby (Easy moves at random, Normal plays carefully). If someone leaves mid-game the computer plays their colour until they come back.
