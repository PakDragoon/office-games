office games

Quick multiplayer games for a break with your colleagues. Free, nothing to install, no sign-up.

One person hosts and gets a 4-letter code; everyone else joins with it, on a laptop or a phone.

  • Melon Burst

    NEW

    Guess the word letter by letter. Every wrong letter snaps another rubber band round your watermelon, and one too many makes it burst.

    • 1 to 8 players
    • Race, or pick words 1v1 / 2v2
    • Daily word and practice
    Play Melon Burst

  • Flipchip

    NEW

    Trap a line of your opponent’s chips between two of yours and they all flip to your colour. Most chips when the board fills up wins.

    • 2 players, others watch
    • Board game
    • Play the computer too
    Play Flipchip
  • ROTT EN

    Rotten

    NEW

    Type a word with the letters to pass the banana on before it splats. Nobody knows when it will go off, so be quick. Last one standing wins.

    • 2 to 8 players
    • Words, English or Roman Urdu
    • Daily puzzle and practice
    Play Rotten
  • THE LIAR

    The Liar

    NEW

    Everyone draws the same secret word, one line at a time. One of you doesn’t know it and is bluffing with every stroke. Find the liar.

    • 3 to 8 players
    • Drawing and bluffing
    • About 5 minutes a game
    Play The Liar

  • Guesstimate

    Count the dots, track the ball, eyeball the angle, split the line, time seconds in your head. Everyone gets the same puzzle; closest guess wins.

    • 1 to 8 players
    • Daily puzzle and practice
    • About 5 minutes
    Play Guesstimate
  • THUL

    Thulla

    The Pakistani card game. Get rid of your cards before your friends do. Can’t follow suit? Hit them with a thulla.

    • 2 to 8 players
    • Cards
    • About 10 minutes a round
    Play Thulla
  • WORDLE

    Wordle Party

    Race your friends on the same Wordle word. Watch their colours fill in live, never their letters.

    • Up to 5 players
    • Words
    • A few minutes a round
    Play Wordle Party
